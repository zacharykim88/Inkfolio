v1.3.0 | October 30, 2026

A lock-screen plugin for KOReader. Reading cards show book covers, Douban ratings, reading progress, time, pages, and word counts, with reading goals, Douban monthly rankings, local weather, and screenshots.

Choose Receipt, Pixel, Modern, or Cover, adjust the appearance with live previews, and switch between Simplified Chinese and English.

Compatibility
• Kobo Sage and Kindle: tested on real devices.
• PocketBook: requires KOReader; sleep screens depend on device support. Not tested on real devices.
• Android phones and e-readers: previews and screenshots may work with official KOReader builds that support user patches. Cannot replace the system lock screen. Not tested on real devices.
• F-Droid builds of KOReader do not support the required user patches.
